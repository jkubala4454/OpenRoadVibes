# OpenRoadVibes Vision  
*A global, open, and community‑driven understanding of road quality.*

---

## 🌱 Why OpenRoadVibes Exists

Road quality affects everyone — drivers, cyclists, delivery workers, emergency responders, and entire communities.  
Yet real‑world road roughness data is often:

- Expensive to collect  
- Locked behind proprietary systems  
- Inaccessible to the public  
- Inconsistent across regions  
- Outdated or incomplete  

OpenRoadVibes exists to change that.

We believe that **road quality should be measurable, transparent, and openly available**, and that modern low‑cost sensors make this possible for the first time.

---

## 🚗 What We’re Building

OpenRoadVibes is creating a **global, open‑source platform** for sensing, analyzing, and mapping road roughness using affordable hardware and community participation.

Our vision includes:

- A **low‑cost ESP32‑based device** that anyone can build  
- A **standardized open data format** for roughness measurements  
- A **server pipeline** that aggregates and normalizes crowdsourced data  
- A **public map** showing roughness levels on roads worldwide  
- A **community ecosystem** that grows and improves the project over time  

This is not just a tool — it’s an open infrastructure for understanding the world’s roads.

---

## 🧠 Guiding Principles

### **1. Open by Default**
All hardware designs, firmware, data formats, and software are open‑source.  
Transparency builds trust and invites collaboration.

### **2. Accessible to Everyone**
The hardware must remain affordable.  
The software must remain approachable.  
The data must remain free.

### **3. Community‑Driven**
The project grows through shared effort.  
Contributors shape the roadmap, tools, and standards.

### **4. Science‑Informed, Not Certification‑Bound**
We draw inspiration from established metrics like IRI, but we do not aim to replace certified profilers.  
Our focus is **relative roughness**, not regulatory compliance.

### **5. Privacy‑Respecting**
We collect only what is necessary.  
We avoid personally identifiable information.  
We design systems that protect contributors.

### **6. Built for the Long Term**
The architecture should be modular, maintainable, and scalable.  
The project should outlive any single contributor.

---

## 🛣️ What Success Looks Like

OpenRoadVibes succeeds when:

- Anyone can build a device for under $40  
- Contributors around the world upload roughness data  
- A public map shows real‑time road quality  
- Cyclists can find smoother routes  
- Drivers can avoid rough roads  
- Cities can identify maintenance needs  
- Researchers can study road conditions at scale  
- The community feels ownership of the project  

Success is not measured in downloads — it’s measured in **shared knowledge**.

---

## 🔭 Long‑Term Vision

Looking ahead, OpenRoadVibes aims to:

- Become the **world’s largest open roughness dataset**  
- Support advanced analytics (pseudo‑IRI, ML‑based classification)  
- Integrate with navigation tools and cycling apps  
- Enable municipalities to use open data for planning  
- Inspire new hardware variations and sensor modules  
- Foster a global network of contributors and maintainers  

We imagine a future where road quality is as easy to check as the weather — and where that information is created by the people who use the roads every day.
